What to Bring

Don’t Forget These The lodge does not supply towels or toiletries. Bring your own.
Bed linens are provided, so you don’t need sheets.

Pack for certain

  • Bath towels
  • Shampoo, soap, and anything else you use
  • Something a little nicer for Saturday dinner, business casual. Mike’s taking the family photo that day.

Worth throwing in

  • Bathing suit and a towel for the hot tub
  • Sneakers — there’s a full pickleball and basketball court
  • Sunscreen and bug spray, for eight acres of woods
  • A camp chair, if you have room
  • A flashlight for the fire pit after dark

For Kay

  • Your questions for Kay
  • An instrument, if you play. We’re singing Dona Nobis Pacem at some point, and Ed is bringing a guitar.
